Time vs counts, per commit

Any two asv benchmarks of this suite, one per y-axis (full asv site). Series are read from asv's index.json, so renamed or new benchmarks appear here without changing the page. Counts are exact under PYTHONHASHSEED=0.

Change per commit

Each point is one commit against the previous measured one: % change of the right-axis series (x) against % change of the left-axis series (y). On the diagonal the right series explains the left; on the y-axis the left moved without the right; on the x-axis the right moved without the left. Commits that change neither sit at the origin. Click a point for the commit.

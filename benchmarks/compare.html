Time vs counts, per commit

From the asv benchmark Correlation.track_value (full asv site). The time is the median of its samples (stream: cold passes in fresh processes; families: warm, fresh engine). Counts are exact under PYTHONHASHSEED=0.

Change per commit

Each point is one commit against the previous measured one: % change of the right-axis metric (x) against % change of the left-axis metric (y). On the diagonal the count explains the time; on the y-axis the time moved without the count; on the x-axis the count moved without the time. Commits that change neither sit at the origin.

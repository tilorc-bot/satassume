## Reading

- After 2.5, evictions are not where sessions come from: 143 of 1,597 builds,
  about 76 ms logged. At about 0.4 ms each, a 32-slot LRU would save 44 builds
  (about 0.4% of the replay) and a 64-slot LRU 75 builds (about 0.7%). That
  is too little to re-test `keep_sessions` unless 1.2 changes the per-session
  cost picture. The plan's retest condition ("if evictions dominate") is not
  met.
- Cone rebuilds dominate what remains: 757 builds and 1,348 ms logged
  (about 31% of the logged pass, search included). Whatever 1.2 does to cone
  search decides the next session question, and 2.2 (clone) only matters if
  cones survive 1.2.
- First-sight builds (444, about 7% logged) are irreducible by caching. Only
  cheaper construction (2.2, 2.3) helps them.

count it would not clear 5% anyway.

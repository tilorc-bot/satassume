# Agent report: item 1.3, phase heuristic (measured, dropped)

- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Status:** dropped by the plan's first rule (ceiling under 5% of the
  replay); nothing implemented. Script
  `agent-reports/scripts/phase_bound.py`, committed on `perf-solver` with
  this report.
- **Scope:** `satassume/solver.py` (`_pick_branch`, `_polarity`),
  unchanged
- **Read this if:** you wonder whether a smarter phase choice (relation
  atoms decided last; the phase satisfying more watched clauses) could
  make the refine-stream searches cheaper

## Measurement

Offline from the 0.3 log: 6,384 solves in 3,192 searching queries
(3,152 logged ms); decisions per solve median 11, p90 22, 81,320 in all;
462 conflicts in all, 409 solves see any; the 387 queries with a
conflicting solve are 594 ms, 18.9% of the search queries' time (which
includes their propagation and escalation stages).

Online, one instrumented pass (`phase_bound.py`; `_pick_branch`,
`_theory_conflict` and `_solve` wrapped under `tools/query_log.py`'s
wrappers; answers identical): 1,193 ms in the 6,384 entails solves;
74,847 decisions, 4,972 of them on theory atoms (all in the 1,564
theory-session solves, which take 559 ms and make 23,901 decisions);
266 theory conflicts, 187 of them right after a decision on a theory
atom, 462 conflicts in all.

**Ceiling:** a phase heuristic can only remove conflicts and the decisions
that lead to them, so its ceiling is the whole time of the solves that
see any conflict: 409 solves, **207 ms = 17.3% of the solve time = 4.9%
of the 4.22 s cold replay**, and that only if those solves became free,
which they cannot (they still need their decisions and propagation; a
conflict-free solve of the same size costs most of that). The plan's
variant (a), relation atoms decided last, targets the 187 conflicts that
follow a theory-atom decision: they sit in 219 solves worth 113 ms, 2.7%
of the replay as a ceiling. Variant (b), the phase satisfying more
unsatisfied watched clauses, would cost a scan of the watch lists on
every one of the 74,847 decisions, which is more work than the 462
conflicts it could save.

## Decision

Dropped: ceiling 4.9% of the replay, real bound well below it. No code
change, no A/B, gate2 or test run.

## Risks for review

- The ceiling counts whole solves; a per-conflict cost would be tighter
  still. Nothing here can push the bound above the ceiling.
- `phase_bound.py` shares the entails-solve tagging of
  `tools/query_log.py` (last literal of `_solve`); same maintenance note
  as the 1.1 and 1.2 scripts.

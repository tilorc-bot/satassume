# Agent report: perf round 2, item 2.3, rule block vs structural templates (measurement only)

- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Status:** measurement only; no code. The decision (implement the rules
  propagator at 10% or more) is the orchestrator's.
- **Scope:** exclusive wall time and clause counts of the per-node
  materialization paths, one cold replay pass on the Pi, branch at `f84febf`
  (3.2, 3.3 and 2.5 applied).
- **Read this if:** you decide on 2.3 or implement it.

## Method

This was an ad hoc wrapper script, not committed. It wraps `Solver.add_pattern`
(called only from `Session.node` with `RULE_INTERNAL`, the single-node rule
base), `Session._emit_pattern`, `Session._compile_patterns`,
`Session._compile`, `TemplateRegistry.clauses_for`, `Session.node`,
`Session.assume_formula` and `Session.query_literal`. It records exclusive
time (the nested wrapped calls subtracted) with `perf_counter`, plus clause
counts. There were two runs, both with every answer matching. The wrapped pass
takes 3.79 s against 3.63 s unwrapped (about 4% overhead, spread over about
80k wrapped calls).

## Reading

- **Materializing the unary rule block costs 11.2% of the cold pass.** The
  templates cost 19.7% (12.8 + 5.2 + 1.7). So the unary-rule share clears
  the plan's 10% threshold, but only just, and the wrapper overhead inflates
  it slightly (its 8,260 calls add well under 0.01 s).
- This counts emission only. The rule clauses are also 79% of the clause
  database the solver propagates over inside `query_literal` (44.5%). A
  propagator would change that cost too, in either direction: fewer watch
  visits, but a Python callback per assignment. It was not measured, because
  it needs solver internals (which clause is the reason of each propagation)
  and `solver.py` is not mine. The solver agent could count it with a few
  read-only lines, or with a wrapper on `_propagate` that classifies reasons
  by clause identity.
- The node-visit bookkeeping (6.4%) would mostly stay: a propagator still
  needs the node's variable block and its cached-fact units.

## Decision

Measurement only: rule-block materialization is 11.2% of the cold pass (at
or just above the 10% threshold), plus an unmeasured share of propagation.
The orchestrator decides.

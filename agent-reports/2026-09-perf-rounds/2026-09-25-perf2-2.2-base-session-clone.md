- **Date:** 2026-09-25
- **Status:** dropped by the plan's first rule (bound 1.6% of the cold
  pass; 5.1% even with a free clone). Nothing implemented; no
  `Solver.clone()`, no engine glue spec. Script
  `agent-reports/2026-09-perf-rounds/scripts/clone_bound.py`, committed on `perf-solver` with

  included), cold pass 3.59 s locally.
- **Scope:** `satassume/solver.py` (a clone), `satassume/engine.py`
  (`_fresh_session`, `Session.assume_formula`, the cone block of

- **Read this if:** you want to know what a cone rebuild actually spends
  on re-grounding the assumptions, and why cloning a per-assumption-set
  base session cannot pay

## Measurement

A cone rebuild starts from `Engine._fresh_session()` and
`Session.assume_formula(assumptions)`: the assumption formula grounded
through SymPy, its nodes created with their rule blocks and templates,
relation atoms registered with the theories. That is what a clone of a
per-assumption-set base session would replace. The query's own cone
(`_literal`, `escalate`, the search) is per cone and stays. `clone_bound.py`
(stage tracking from `tools/query_log.py` underneath; answers identical)
times both calls per build, and estimates the clone's own cost on the
base state at the end of `assume_formula`: a prototype `Solver` clone
(every field copied, clauses and watches remapped through an id map,
learnt clauses re-created; theories not cloned) plus shallow copies of
every container attribute of the `Session`, its `VarTable`, its
`Relations` and adapters.

| build kind | queries | with a theory | `_fresh_session` + `assume_formula` | clone estimate | net | share of cold pass | base solver: vars / clauses |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| cone rebuild | 757 | 64 | 166.4 ms | 110.9 ms | 55.5 ms | 1.5% | 53 / 127 |
| first sight | 444 | 65 | 123.4 ms | 117.3 ms | 6.1 ms | (not replaceable) | 66 / 159 |
| evicted | 143 | 1 | 17.4 ms | 14.3 ms | 3.1 ms | 0.1% | 42 / 100 |

The base build costs 0.22 ms (cone) to 0.28 ms (first) per session, the
prototype clone 0.15 to 0.26 ms: a base session is small (about 50
variables, 130 clauses; the SymPy grounding, not the solver, is most of
the build) and copying it is not much cheaper than building it. **Bound:
59 ms, 1.6% of the cold pass; 184 ms, 5.1%, if the clone were free.**
With 2.5 in place the failed builds are gone, so the evicted class is
143 builds, 0.5% even before the clone cost.

Where a cone rebuild's time goes instead: the 2.1 census put cone
queries at about 31% of the logged time pre-2.5; the base build is 166
ms of that. The rest is the query's own cone (escalation instantiating
the cone's nodes, rule blocks included: see the 2.3 measurement, where
rule-block insertion is 10% of the pass) and the search.

## Decision

Dropped: 1.6% (5.1% ceiling with a free clone). No solver change, no
clone operation in the fuzz, no engine glue spec, no A/B, gate2 or test
run. The theory clone that 64 of the 757 cone builds would also need is
moot.

## Risks for review

- The clone estimate is a prototype in the script, not a tuned
  implementation; a tuned one could be somewhat faster than 0.15 ms per
  base session, but the ceiling with a free clone is what bounds the
  item, and it is 5.1% only by counting every cone and evicted build.
- `_fresh_session` on this HEAD is `Session(self)` (a `Solver()`, a
  `VarTable`, empty dicts); its time is included and negligible.

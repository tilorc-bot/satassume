## Measurement

The 0.3 log holds every solve's model but not the assumption and target
literals of the `entails` solves, so the question "would an earlier model
have satisfied A ∧ ¬P" was answered on the replay itself:
`agent-reports/2026-09-perf-rounds/scripts/witness_bound.py` wraps `Solver._solve`, keeps a
ring of the last *k* models per solver (one solver per session) and,
before each real solve, tests every stored model (most recent first)
against the call's literals, the problem clauses added since the model
was found (an index slice of `_clauses`), the root literals fixed since
(a trail slice; unit clauses never reach `_clauses`) and, with theories
attached, the plan's conservative gate (dropped if an atom was registered
since). A passing model is a hit: that solve was unnecessary. The real
solve still runs (answers identical), its wall time is what the hit would
save, and its model is not added to the ring, so the ring evolves as in an
implementation. Models never cross sessions (each session has its own
variable table), so "under the same assumption set" across sessions is
not a source of witnesses here.

Local machine (2 cores), `PYTHONHASHSEED=0`; instrumented pass 4.42 s,
uninstrumented cold replay 4.22 s; 6,384 solves (3,192 `not_p`, 3,192
`p`), 1.13 s inside `_solve` (25.7% of the instrumented pass):

| ring | hits of 6,384 solves | `not_p` / `p` | None searches fully answered (of 3,130) | solve time saved | check time | net share of the pass |
|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 673 (10.5%) | 673 / 0 | 0 | 0.088 s | 0.012 s | 1.7% |
| 2 | 1,359 (21.3%) | 840 / 519 | 400 | 0.194 s | 0.008 s | 4.2% |
| 4 | 1,410 (22.1%) | 864 / 546 | 451 | 0.201 s | 0.006 s | 4.4% |
| 8 | 1,414 (22.1%) | 864 / 550 | 455 | 0.202 s | 0.006 s | 4.4% |
| 16 | 1,414 (22.1%) | 864 / 550 | 455 | 0.202 s | 0.006 s | 4.4% |

Hits by age of the model (ring 8): 864 most recent, 497 second, 44
third, 9 older: two models per solver capture nearly everything. Hits on
an unsat solve: 0 (the check is sound). Without the theory gate (ring 16):
1,425 hits, 0.206 s, 4.5%: the gate costs nothing. The 14 hits on `False`
queries and 0 on `True` are the `not_p`/`p` solve that is satisfiable in
those searches; no `True`/`False` answer is short-cut as a whole.

**Bound: 0.20 s of a 4.22 s replay, 4.8% at best** (4.4% of the
instrumented pass; the check cost is included, and skipping a solve would
change nothing else, since the hit solves are conflict-free ones whose
learnt clauses do not exist). Hit solves are cheaper than average: 22% of
the solves are 18% of the solve time.

Why so few: a stored model of A ∧ ¬P₁ answers a later A ∧ ¬P₂ only if
P₂ is false in it, and A ∧ P₂ only if it is true; with the negative
default phase most model literals are false, which is why `not_p` hits
(864) outnumber `p` hits (550) and why the `p` side needs the second-most
recent model (the last `p` model). Per session with 4 or more solves (51
sessions): median hit share 0.23, p25 0.16, p75 0.27.

## The plan's follow-up

"If most Nones of a session share one model, reconsider `entails` itself:
find a model of A first and read P off it." They do not: even an unbounded
ring answers only 455 of the 3,130 `None` searches completely, and a
single model covers 10.5% of the solves. `entails` already does the
one-model variant implicitly (its first solve, A ∧ ¬P, is a model of A
with P read off as false; the second solve is needed exactly because P is
not forced), so a "model of A first" rewrite is the ring of size 1 with a
free choice of P's phase, bounded above by the 21% of ring 2. No.

Pi (`witness_bound.py` runs in 5 s; the shares should match within the
1% the two machines agree to).

## Risks for review

- The saved time is the real solves' wall time on this machine; the check
  time is Python-level per ring size and slightly pessimistic (five rings
  were checked in one pass, each timed separately).
- The simulation ignores second-order effects of a skipped solve on later
  ones (phase saving, activities); the hit solves have 0 conflicts, so no
  learnt clause is lost.
- `witness_bound.py` copies the target-tagging logic of
  `tools/query_log.py` (last literal of `_solve` against `entails`'
  target); it needs the same update if `entails` changes.
